## 💡 About MyFolio

**MyFolio** solves developer portfolio fatigue. Traditional portfolio builders trap engineers in repetitive, cookie-cutter templates with unmotivated purple gradients and fake developer metrics. 

MyFolio introduces a **Content-First Generative Architecture**:
- 🎯 **Strict Zero Predefined Template Filling**: The platform parses the developer's real git history, pull requests, and technical case studies to dynamically architect a tailored Information Architecture (IA) model.
- 🌌 **Authentic Spatial Worlds**: Interactive 3D WebGL viewports, procedural lighting, materials, and physics-driven cards tailored to the developer's technical domain.
- 🧬 **Dynamic Section Morphing**: Every section exists only if backed by verifiable proof. Unsupported sections are purged automatically.
- ⚡ **Instant Global Custom Domains & Hosting**: One-click deployment with automatic SSL, zero-watermark hosting, and custom domain mapping.

## 🧠 AI Portfolio Identity & Content Engine

MyFolio generates personalized digital worlds around real people through a strict evidence-driven pipeline:

$$\text{CONTENT} \longrightarrow \text{IDENTITY} \longrightarrow \text{STORY} \longrightarrow \text{HIERARCHY} \longrightarrow \text{SECTION STRUCTURE} \longrightarrow \text{VISUAL REPRESENTATION} \longrightarrow \text{3D EXPERIENCE}$$

- **Zero Fabricated Information**: Never invents employers, false metrics, clients, awards, or claims.
- **Strict Project Title & Domain Relevancy**: Every project card receives artwork semantically matched to the project's title and engineering discipline (e.g. Encrypted Messenger $\to$ cryptographic cipher; AI Reasoning $\to$ autonomous edge neural node; Smart Contracts $\to$ blockchain consensus ledger).
- **Mandatory 3-Tier Typography Engine**: Enforces strict zero-repetition typography cycles matching candidate archetypes (`Orbitron`, `Chakra Petch`, `Cinzel Decorative`, `Playfair Display`, `Syne`, `Syncopate`, `Space Grotesk`, `JetBrains Mono`).
