## 3. Maker's First Comment (Pin as First Post)

```markdown
Hey Product Hunt community! 👋

I'm Abdul Aziz, the creator and lead architect of MyFolio (https://myfolio.tech).

Like many of you, I was tired of seeing the same sterile, cookie-cutter developer portfolios. As engineers, we build complex distributed systems, backend pipelines, and interactive apps—yet our portfolios often look like a flat template from 2015.

I built MyFolio to change that.

### 🌟 What makes MyFolio different?
1. **GitHub Ingestion in 30s**: Simply enter your GitHub username. MyFolio analyzes your actual commit recency, technologies, and READMEs to synthesize authentic technical case studies without hallucinated metrics.
2. **Interactive 3D WebGL Universes**: Choose between 6 spatial worlds powered by Three.js, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and Lenis smooth inertia scrolling:
   - Jack 3D: Cyberpunk engineering sprawl with procedural circuit traces.
   - Nadia: High-contrast architectural editorial luxury.
   - Kage: Serene 3D Kyoto zen garden.
   - Apogee: Deep space orbital telemetry and cosmic starfields.
   - Tom: Hand-drawn 2D/3D cel animation with physics.
   - RBP: Kinetic monochrome brutalism.
3. **100% Source Code ZIP Export (Zero Lock-In)**: We hate SaaS lock-in as much as you do. Every paid build includes full standalone HTML, CSS, JS, and 3D asset downloads. You can self-host it for free on GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or AWS.
4. **Transparent Pricing**: 3 free evaluation builds per week with 24-hour previews. Permanent hosting with full source code download starts at just ₹149 (~$1.80).

I’d love for you to test your GitHub handle in the studio and share your live 3D portfolio preview!

What visual universe should we build next? Let me know in the comments! 🚀
```
