PRODUCTION PIPELINE EXPLAINED

THE 5-STEP GAG MACHINE

From scribble on napkin to full 24fps slapstick payoff. How every sequence travels through the studio to maximize comedic timing, anatomical appeal, and delivery.

01 STORY BEAT

The Spark & Beat Board

Thumbnail doodles exploring rhythm, sight gags, eye-tracking vectors, and the core emotional beats of the character conflict.

Output: 12 Thumbnail Cards
02 TIMING CHART

Slapstick Physics

Plotting the extremes. Setting up squash-and-stretch ratios, hang-time pauses, anticipation holds, and sudden impact frames.

Output: Exposure Sheet (X-Sheet)
03 PENCIL TEST

Rough Keyframes

Raw digital graphite passes testing silhouette clarity, dynamic weight transfer, arcs, and overshoot physics at full frame rate.

Output: 24fps Rough QuickTime
04 INK & BRUSH

Clean-Up & Inking

Tie-down line pass preserving the kinetic looseness while locking volume consistency, expressive tapering, and model-sheet fidelity.

Output: Production Vector/Bitmap Cels
05 FINAL COMP

Color & Sound Pop

Four-color litho fills, paper grain texturing, speed-line composite passes, and sync adjustments against custom Foley and orchestral stings.

Output: 4K Master Deliverable

BIO • ISSUE PROFILE 30 YEARS HOLDING A STYLUS

THE MAN BEHIND THE PENCIL

I am a traditional animation purist trained in Golden Age physical timing. I cut my teeth analyzing frame-by-frame 35mm film strips from 1940s theatrical shorts, transcribing how the masters bent anatomy to convey raw impact, panic, and heart.

Over the last twelve years, I have adapted those timeless principles to modern broadcast, streaming, and game engines. Whether directing a full episodic short or polishing a 3-second comic micro-interaction, my philosophy remains constant: if the silhouette doesn’t read in one frame, the joke doesn’t land.
